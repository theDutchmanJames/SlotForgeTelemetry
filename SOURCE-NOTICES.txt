0.2.0: renamed the user-facing app to SlotForge Telemetry; added independent
telemetry-model.js and telemetry-view.js for weight/ballast, distribution,
acceleration, tyre wear, gear matching and measured lap/scale speed.
The original Paddock performance-model.js remains unchanged.

0.3.0: added local setup library with explicit save/update/copy, names and notes,
search/sort, load/reference, rename/duplicate/delete/undo, validated backup
import/export, storage failure handling and integration tests.

0.4.0: four-page Telemetry / Analyze / Library / Settings redesign, saved-only
analysis and comparisons, expanded calculation help and remembered light/dark
themes. renderer/art/*.svg are native vector illustrations: gear and rim paths
derive from renderer/icons/spur-gear.svg and wheel-rim.svg; motor geometry,
shading and axle composition are new. Design mockups remain raster references.

Linux/macOS local builder workflow inspired by SlotForge Timing build-linux.sh,
build-mac.command and their quick variants (main, inspected 2026-11-01).
Telemetry implements a shared locked-dependency builder and retains its own
profile, stopwatch icon and package targets.
