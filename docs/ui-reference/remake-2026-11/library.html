Library, remade

The Library has two rooms that do not talk to each other. Saved setups is a table of name, gearing, tyres and weight, with a detail card whose picture is the same chassis for every setup and eight buttons. The Garage has car tiles with an empty grey box, "2 setup(s) · 7 run(s)", a TELEMETRY tag on every card, and tracks as a separate table. Every setup has a car, yet nothing is grouped by it.

  • Replaces: Library › Saved setups and Library › Garage (cars and tracks).
  • Kept: everything you can do today: open on the workbench, rename, duplicate, delete, setup sheet, import and export the library, Paddock cars with their photos, your own cars, tracks with lane lengths, "New setup for this car", "Track day with this car", search and sort.
  • New, each one for you to approve: setups grouped by car, what changed between one setup and the next of the same car, the best lap and record per lane on each track, a favourite star, and setups without a car gathered in one place.

A · Car Garage

Visual, the car is the way in, like walking into your garage.

  • The Library opens on your cars as big cards: photo, setups, runs, best lap.
  • A car opens its own page: facts on the left, its setups as a timeline, each with what changed against the one before.
  • Tracks are cards with every lane's length and record.
  • Setups without a car wait in one line at the bottom.

B · Setup Binder

Dense and quick, like a well-kept binder: everything in one list.

  • One list of all setups, in a section per car, with gearing, tyre, weight, top speed and best lap as columns.
  • Tick several for one action bar: compare, export, delete.
  • The selected setup as a spec card on the right, with where it was driven.
  • Cars and tracks below in a manager: a list on the left, the form on the right.
