Connect to Paddock, remade

Connect to Paddock is plumbing shown as a form: "Paddock on this computer" with a button and a grey "Not connected." box, then "Paddock on another computer" with three numbered steps, Find on this network, an address field, a password field and four buttons (Connect, Disconnect, Import / refresh results, and an archive button when something breaks), and three paragraphs about formats, ports and passwords. What you want to know (am I connected, is my data up to date, what do I do if not) has no place of its own.

  • Replaces: the Settings › Connect to Paddock page and its status line.
  • Kept: both ways in (this computer without a password; another computer with Dealership Mode and the password), finding Paddock on the network, the remembered collection, never saving the password, refresh, disconnect and rebuilding an unreadable cache.
  • New, each one for you to approve: what came in (races, cars, photos) with counts and when, a short import history, and plain "fix it" steps when it does not work.

A · Status Card

One card that answers "am I connected?", with one button.

  • On top: the status in big type, a light, and one button that does what is needed now: Connect, Refresh, or Fix it.
  • What you have from Paddock: races, cars, photos, last import.
  • Where is Paddock? This computer / Another computer. The network way is three short steps in a row, not a form.
  • A short import history.

B · Connection Map

A picture of the link, and checks that say what is wrong.

  • A map: Telemetry, the link, Paddock. The line is green and moving when connected, grey when not; what comes over it is written on it.
  • Checks below run by themselves: is Paddock running, is "Paddock on this computer" on, is the collection readable. A failed check says what to do.
  • The network way and the connection details sit on the right.
