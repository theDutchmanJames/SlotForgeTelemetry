The workbench, below the setup bar

The setup bar and the four view tabs were redone earlier. Below them the workbench still has two lists that do not touch: Setup inputs on the left as folders, Live results on the right as other folders with Pin buttons. You type a pinion on the left and look for its effect in a different folder on the right. At the bottom, "Ready to look deeper?" and "How does your car behave?" are adverts for other pages.

  • Replaces: the two lists (input folders and live result folders with Pin), the drivetrain picture, the "useful starting point" note and the two advert cards.
  • Kept: every input and every result, the view tabs (Quick setup, Measure the car, Plan & test, Everything), the help next to each input, the gear chart, and "How are these numbers calculated".
  • New, each one for you to approve: each input shown next to what it changes, and what changed since you last saved, as figures ("+3.00 km/h since saved").

A · Paired Rows

One row per topic: what you enter on the left, what it does on the right.

  • Gearing: pinion and gear left; ratio, rollout, top speed and a small gear chart right.
  • The same for motor, tyres, weight, and the measuring topics.
  • A row closes to one summary line, so the page stays short.
  • At the bottom: what to do next, as three quiet tiles with your own data in them.

B · Cockpit

A strip of the six figures that matter, always in view while you type.

  • A dashboard at the top: ratio, top speed, pull, power-to-weight, weight, clearance. The figures your typing changes light up, with the change since you saved.
  • Inputs below as one tidy form, a section per topic.
  • Click a figure for the detail on the right: how it is made, the full table, which inputs feed it.
