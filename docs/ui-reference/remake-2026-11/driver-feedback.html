Driver Feedback, remade

Driver Feedback starts from what you feel in the car, which is right. But it opens as a five-step wizard: a drop-down "What would you like help with?", a second drop-down for the behaviour, three folded sections, a box that says "Start with an observation, not a specification sheet", and only then a button for your first checks. The checks themselves and what to try sit two steps further.

  • Replaces: the five steps and their side list (Your experience, First checks, Your setup, Look deeper, Try & learn).
  • Kept: every symptom and every follow-up question, the six checks (guide, tyres, axles, body, pickup, the corner itself) with "looks OK / problem found", the optional measurements, the research behind each suggestion, and saving all of it with the setup.
  • New, each one for you to approve: pictures instead of drop-downs, answers as buttons, and a suggestion handed to Track day to test ("Try it on Track day").

A · Symptom Cards

One page that grows as you answer, like a conversation.

  • Start: ten picture cards, one per behaviour (tipping, rear sliding, guide escape, hopping…), plus "not sure".
  • Each follow-up question is a row of answer buttons under it, never a drop-down.
  • Then the checks as a to-do list: looks OK / problem found.
  • Then three things to try, best first, each with why and a button to test it on Track day.

B · Case File

Like a notebook per problem: where it happens, and everything you tried.

  • A corner map: tap where it happens (entry, apex, exit, straight, a joint), then what the car does.
  • Each problem becomes a case with its own file: the facts on the right, then a log of checks and changes, with "it helped / no change".
  • Several cases side by side as tabs: "rear slides at the hairpin", "hops on the back straight".
