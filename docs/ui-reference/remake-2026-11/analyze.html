Analyze, remade

Analyze has two halves today. Analysis shows a saved setup's results again, the same numbers the workbench shows live, behind a picker and eight topics. Compare is a 20-row table of two setups where 13 rows read "Unavailable", and you have to work out yourself what you changed. This remake makes Analyze about one question: what does this change do?

  • Replaces: Analyze › Analysis and Analyze › Compare, and Library's "Compare with…". The tab becomes Setups. A single setup's results stay on the workbench (all eight topics, live), and "Analyze" on a saved setup opens it there read-only.
  • Kept: every comparison number of today (speed, pulling force, rollout, ratio, power, power-to-weight, weight, lap speed, magnetic downforce, chassis geometry and balance), the "same basis" rules (no comparing power across different test voltages), and the torque and power curves.
  • New, each one for you to approve: up to three setups at once, the workbench as it is now as one of them (compare before you save), setups grouped by car, and what you changed listed first.

A · Change Sheet

Words and numbers, like an engineer's change note.

  • Left: your setups by car. Pin one as the base, tick up to two more, or the workbench now.
  • On top: what you changed, input by input.
  • Then a plain verdict, and what it does: only the results that moved, with a bar for up or down.
  • Unchanged results and ones that need measurements fold away into one line each.

B · Dyno Room

Chart-first, like a rolling road: you see the setups pull.

  • On top: the setups as coloured chips; switch them on and off. The workbench now is a dashed line.
  • The dyno chart: how hard each setup pulls its own weight at every speed, a crosshair you slide to read them at any speed.
  • Beside it, where each one wins: top speed, pull out of a corner, power-to-weight.
  • Below: a spec card per setup, with the values that differ marked.
