Track day, remade

Track day is the page you use with the car in your hand. Today it opens with four free text fields (car, track, lane, driver), a copy of the workbench, an empty text box for "what did you change", another for the lap times, and the best part, compare with last run, squeezed into the right column. Nothing knows it is one session, and you type the change by hand while the workbench already knows it.

  • Replaces: the Track day page (context fields, current setup, log a run, compare with last run, today's runs).
  • Kept: lap entry by typing or pasting (seconds or minutes:seconds), the "in order, no laps missing" choice, the comparison with the run before (best, typical, spread, laps), runs saved as Track day runs that land on the Laps wall.
  • New, each one for you to approve: car, track and lane picked from your garage; the change picked from the setup (pinion 9 → 10) and written to the workbench for you; a session that holds today's runs; and in option B a tap timer and a test plan.

A · Pit Board

Every run side by side, like the board at the pit wall.

  • On top: the session: car, track, lane and driver, picked from your garage.
  • Each run is a column: its change, typical lap in big figures, best, spread, its laps, and the verdict against the run before. The quickest is marked.
  • The last column is the next run: change the setup with steppers (pinion, gear, weight, tyre), type laps one box at a time; the verdict appears as you type.
  • When a change wins: Keep this change saves it to the workbench setup.

B · Test Plan

A guided test with a timer, like a test session with an engineer.

  • Left: a test plan: baseline, the change, then the baseline again to make sure the track did not change.
  • Centre: a tap timer: press Space (or the big button) when the car crosses the line; the lap times fill in by themselves. Typing or pasting stays possible.
  • Right: the verdict gauge: quicker or slower, and whether both baseline runs agree.
  • At the end: keep the change, or go back to the baseline.
