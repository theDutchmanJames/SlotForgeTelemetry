What is this number?
Rollout

How far the car moves for one turn of the motor. The bigger the rollout, the faster but the less pull.

rollout = π × tyre ÷ ratio
= π × 20 mm ÷ 2.7
= 23.27 mm per motor turn

With your 10 / 27 gearing and 20 mm tyres the car moves 23.27 mm per motor turn. With 9 teeth it was 20.94 mm: 11% less distance per turn, so more pull out of corners.

Assumes no tyre slip and a single-stage drivetrain. Worn tyres roll out less: at 19 mm it is 22.11 mm.
