**SlotForge Telemetry 0.9.0 adds magnetic downforce measurements and eleven tools for preparing, testing and understanding your slotcars.**

### Magnetic downforce

Record repeated magnetic-pull readings and the tester, rails, mounting and motor conditions. See mean pull, repeatability, force in newtons, pull relative to car weight and total static support load. Add measured clearance points to explore a graph, interpolation within the measured range, tyre-wear scenarios and margins against an entered club limit.

Magnetic pull remains separate from actual car mass. It does not claim to predict grip, cornering speed or lap times.

### Eleven new Testing & planning tools

Open **Telemetry → Testing & planning** to choose a tool and see live results:

1. **Tyre preparation & life:** target diameter, gear-clearance limits, left/right matching, diameter variation and remaining-life estimates from measured wear.
2. **Electrical losses:** loaded voltage drop, effective resistance and power loss from confirmed steady-DC measurements.
3. **Motor test records:** individual motor measurements, torque-arm conversion, simultaneous loaded power and supported efficiency calculations.
4. **Race strategy:** expected race duration, incident and pit-stop losses, and the laps needed to recover an extra stop.
5. **Ballast planning:** multiple added or removed parts, axle or guide support, static balance and ballast needed for a target rear share.
6. **Controlled A–B–A tests:** chronological test runs, representative pace, consistency, incident observations and return-to-baseline drift.
7. **Class limits:** entered measurement requirements, signed margins and clear within/outside results.
8. **Compound gears & geometry:** multi-stage ratios and standard external spur-gear dimensions.
9. **Rotating mass:** equivalent inertia mass and ideal acceleration using declared component geometry.
10. **Braking & coast-down:** measured speed/time traces, distance and deceleration without extrapolating beyond the recorded window.
11. **Measurement ranges:** see how entered measurement bounds affect calculated outputs.

Save the inputs with your setup and open **Analyze → Testing & planning** for saved tables and graphs. Each tool has a dedicated calculation guide, worked example and limitations under Settings. Existing setups remain compatible, and the new measurements are included in setup exports and library backups.

### Use your recorded laps

Controlled tests can copy a window of up to 100 valid, non-pit laps from a manual or Paddock run. Snapshots record their source and date, preserve slow valid laps, and leave original records untouched. Later source corrections do not silently alter a historical test. Comparison conditions must be confirmed explicitly.

### Presentation and reliability

- Theme-aware SlotForge wordmarks in the header and footer, with the existing text alignment preserved.
- Category colours, clear navigation, light/dark layouts and measurement tables throughout the new tools.
- Unknown values stay distinct from measured zero; unsupported calculations remain unavailable.
- Invalid drafts retain the last valid workspace; contradictory or non-finite measurements receive clear feedback.
- Reviewed with 110 automated tests and eight isolated desktop workflow suites before release packaging.

### Updating

Back up your setup library and manual laps, close the old app, then install or open the new version. Existing profiles, setups, lap data and themes are retained. Keep backups before opening new measurement setups in an older version, which does not understand these optional fields.

Windows is unsigned; macOS is ad-hoc signed and not notarized. See the [installation guide](https://github.com/theDutchmanJames/SlotForgeTelemetry#how-to-install) for first-launch instructions.
