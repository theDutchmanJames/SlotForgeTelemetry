**SlotForge Telemetry 0.10.0 adds practical setup guidance, chassis geometry and ballast planning, with clearer measurement inputs and reviewed calculations.**

### Predicted Performance

Open **Analyze → Predicted Performance** to start with what your car does: tipping, sliding, losing the guide, difficult braking or inconsistent laps. Every input is optional. Explore six areas covering handling, drivetrain, tyres, power, pace and mechanical checks.

The advice uses your observations and the current Telemetry workbench, including unsaved edits. It explains what your data supports, what is still missing and which practical test to try next. Stock specifications produce relevant numerical results without being treated as proof of grip or handling. Suggestions preserve your intended motor class; there is no universal performance score or promised lap-time improvement.

### Chassis geometry and ballast planning

- Scaled guide/rear-contact diagrams, explicit weighing arrangements, repeated support readings and horizontal centre-of-gravity calculations.
- Add, remove or move ballast in two dimensions; explore rear weight share, class mass margins and measurement bounds.
- Compare lowering existing mass or adding ballast, changing gearing, and compatible motor-pull measurements without overwriting your measured setup.
- Optional component mass/CG/inertia calculations and magnetic-pull measurements at different car angles.

Static geometry and magnetic measurements describe the entered setup; they do not establish a safe cornering speed or predict a deslot.

### Clearer inputs and useful test evidence

- Examples now sit **outside input fields**, labelled “Example only”. Empty, partial and entered measurement records are distinguished.
- Guidance asks for the next useful missing measurement or confirmation instead of requesting data already supplied.
- Free-text notes are clearly separated from measurements. Manual-lap examples follow the selected units and paste format.
- Controlled A–B–A tests can freeze drivetrain, chassis, workshop and prediction details alongside manual or Paddock lap snapshots. Later setup edits do not rewrite historical evidence.
- Approved light/dark SVG branding retains the app icon and slogan.

### Calculation and reliability fixes

Actual gear clearance is calculated independently of optional targets: a **19 mm tyre with an 18 mm gear gives 0.5 mm clearance**. Unsupported targets no longer produce oversized tyre recommendations or negative wear allowances. Gear protrusion is distinct from clearance, and tyre-life estimates start from the fitted diameter.

The review also tightened ballast suggestions and part-movement checks, corrected incompatible-rating comparisons, and made uninterrupted lap statistics respect timestamp gaps. Missing measurements remain distinct from measured zero.

### Updating

Export backups of your setup library and manual laps, close the old app, then open or install the new version. Existing profiles, setups, lap data and themes are retained. Older versions may not understand the new optional fields; keep your backups before moving setups between versions.

Windows is unsigned; macOS is ad-hoc signed and not notarized. See the [installation guide](https://github.com/theDutchmanJames/SlotForgeTelemetry#how-to-install) for first-launch instructions.
