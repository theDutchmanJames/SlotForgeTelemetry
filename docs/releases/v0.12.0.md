**SlotForge Telemetry 0.12.0 makes Driver Feedback more specific to your car, your measurements and what you have already tried.**

### Advice that uses your setup

Driver Feedback now connects body mass, support geometry, ballast plans, tyre measurements, magnetic pull, gearing, electrical tests, braking traces and lap comparisons to practical suggestions. Each suggestion explains the evidence, the trade-offs and how to check the result.

For example, a ballast plan that reduces front support load is highlighted during a guide-escape investigation. Faster test laps are considered alongside increased incidents. A tyre change followed by wheel lift prompts a check of the grip/roll trade-off before adding more grip.

### One useful question at a time

The next question adapts to the driving symptom and information already supplied. Start with simple observations, reuse workbench values, then add measurements only when they help. All measurements remain optional.

### Remember what you have checked

- Record individual checks for the guide, tyres, axles, body/pod, pickup and track.
- Link a suggested action to a test and record its outcome.
- Completed checks advance the investigation; unresolved measured problems stay visible.
- Changed setups identify earlier checks as historical until explicitly rechecked.
- Save and export the investigation with your setup.

### Explore the supporting research

Browse 79 handling records through 12 source-linked tuning topics. Search reports, inspect counterexamples and open the original sources. Stock-assumed cars, prepared cars and white-kit builds remain distinguishable. Reports suggest useful checks; they do not establish brand rankings, guaranteed diagnoses or lap-time gains.

### Review and compatibility

Expanded regression coverage checks adaptive advice, missing-input questions, saved investigations, legacy outcomes and light/dark layouts. Review fixes preserve explicit checklist resets, correct research-record identification and require appropriate motor-off/net-force conditions for magnetic angle interpretation.

### Updating

Export backups of your setup library and lap data, close Telemetry, and replace the old application with the new download. Existing local data is retained; older setups receive defaults for the new optional fields. Use this version or later to retain the new individual check records.
