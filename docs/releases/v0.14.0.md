**SlotForge Telemetry 0.14.0 brings the app to the track: a Track day page for the change → drive → compare loop, a Garage for your cars and tracks, automatic backups, and a Windows installer.**

### Track day

Set the car, track, lane and driver once, note what you changed, and type or paste your lap times. A live preview shows best, typical and spread while you type. **Compare with last run** gives a plain verdict, such as "typical pace faster by 0.250 s", with a better/worse table and the setup differences, even before you save.

Saved runs carry a snapshot of the gearing, tyres, weight and RPM, and appear in Lap analysis, Progress, Compare runs and Records like any other lap session.

### Garage

**Library → Garage** holds your cars and tracks.

- Cars from Paddock on this computer arrive with each import: name, maker, class, technical configuration and photo.
- Add your own cars for anything that is not in Paddock, and tracks with their lanes and lane lengths.
- A car's page shows its setups and best lap per track and lane, with buttons to start a new setup or a Track day with it.

Setups can now name their car, and Track day suggests garage cars and tracks and uses the lane length you entered.

### Paddock on this computer

Connect to Paddock on the same computer with one click and no password. Every race in the collection is imported, including races Timing saved to a Shared library. Imports are incremental and verified, Telemetry reconnects when Paddock restarts, and corrections and withdrawals follow Paddock.

### Simpler to use

- The workbench opens on **Quick setup**: gearing, motor, rear tyres and weight. Switch to **Measure the car**, **Plan & test** or **Everything** when you need more. Hidden values are kept and still used.
- Analyze groups its modes into *Setups* and *Track laps*, and dims topics with nothing saved yet.
- **Settings → Units:** metric, imperial or your own mix. Saved files always stay metric.
- A **gear chart** shows rollout, speed or ratio for every pinion and driven gear; click a cell to try that gearing.
- Download a printable **setup sheet** for the workbench or any saved setup.

### Backups and updates

Telemetry now backs up all its data automatically: shortly after start-up, every 12 hours while open and when quitting, keeping the newest 10. You can also copy each backup to a folder of your choice, such as a USB stick or a cloud-synced folder. **Settings → Backups** lets you back up now, save a backup elsewhere and restore one. Every backup is checked before it replaces anything, and your current data is saved first.

Telemetry also tells you when a new version is out. A notice offers **Download**, which opens the release page, or **Not now**. Nothing is downloaded or installed automatically, and **Settings → Updates** lets you check manually or turn the check off.

### Review and compatibility

Local validation passed 244 automated tests, 3 Paddock interoperability tests, all 21 desktop suites and Windows packaging; four environment-dependent tests were skipped locally.

Existing setups and lap data remain readable. On first start, the setup library moves from window storage to plain files in Telemetry's profile folder; the old copy is left in place. Older versions ignore a setup's car.

### Updating

Close Telemetry and replace the old application with the new download, or run the installer. Existing local data is retained. From this version on, Telemetry makes backups for you; export one from an older version first if you want to be safe.

See the [installation instructions](https://github.com/theDutchmanJames/SlotForgeTelemetry#how-to-install) for platform-specific help. Windows and macOS builds are not publisher-signed/notarized; the README explains the operating-system prompts.
